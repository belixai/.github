# belix.ai

**Custom Software | Business Automation | Applied AI**

belix.ai LLC is a technology company based in Boulder, Colorado. We design and develop custom software, business automation systems, and applied AI solutions for small and mid-sized businesses.

Our work combines software engineering, systems integration, and artificial intelligence to build technology that supports real business operations.

## What We Do

- **Custom Software Development** - Business applications, dashboards, portals, APIs, and operational platforms.
- **Applied Artificial Intelligence** - AI-powered applications, intelligent agents, and multi-model integrations.
- **Business Automation** - Workflow orchestration, process automation, and systems integration.
- **AI Governance** - Responsible AI implementation, operational controls, and governance-focused software.
- **Software Product Development** - Design, engineering, and operation of original software platforms.

## Products & Initiatives

### [WorkNode.ai](https://worknode.ai/)

A unified AI workspace designed to bring multiple AI capabilities into a connected environment. WorkNode.ai supports AI conversations, coding workflows, content creation, and project-oriented collaboration.

### [AITerms.help](https://aiterms.help/)

A free, provider-neutral educational resource that makes artificial intelligence terminology more accessible. AITerms.help provides explanations for different experience levels, from beginners to advanced practitioners.

AITerms.help is supported by belix.ai while maintaining its independent educational purpose.

Additional software products and initiatives are developed through the belix.ai product studio.

## Our Approach

We build maintainable software and favor solutions that integrate with existing business systems. Our engineering approach emphasizes clear architecture, operational reliability, responsible AI adoption, and long-term adaptability.
